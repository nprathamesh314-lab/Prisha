## 6. Corporate Social Responsibility (`/csr`)

- **Hero Title:** Empowering Drivers, Strengthening Communities
- **Hero Subtitle:** Prisha Xpress Pvt. Ltd. believes the long-term future of Indian logistics depends on the dignity, health, and economic security of commercial truck drivers.
- **The Six Authentic CSR Initiatives:**
  1. **Free Eye Checkups & Vision Care Camps (Active Ground Initiative):** Conducting recurring vision screenings at Pune transit terminals, providing free corrective glasses and eye health evaluations.
  2. **Driver Children Education Support (Ongoing Welfare Program):** Offering textbook stipends, school stationery kits, and merit-based tuition support for long-distance drivers’ children.
  3. **Defensive Driving & Highway Etiquette Training (Active Ground Initiative):** Regular safety coaching covering highway blind-spots, night driving discipline, safe following distances, and polite highway interactions.
  4. **Respect At Work & Dignity Culture (Ongoing Welfare Program):** Fostering a zero-harassment loading bay environment where drivers are treated with courtesy, professional dignity, and fair queuing.
  5. **Safe Highway Pit Stops for Rest (Ongoing Welfare Program):** Establishing designated resting points along key highways offering clean restrooms, safe vehicle parking, and hydration facilities.
  6. **Assured On-Time Bank Account Settlements (Ongoing Welfare Program):** Guaranteeing prompt, electronic trip remuneration directly into verified bank accounts, eliminating predatory middleman cuts.
